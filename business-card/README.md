# GeoShop Promotional Card Design

A promotional graphic designed for GeoShop Sales & Hire,
a surveying equipment sales and rental platform.

## Design objectives
- Communicate the company's equipment sales and hire services.
- Present key surveying equipment categories.
- Encourage customers to visit the online store through a QR code.
- Maintain consistency with the company's visual identity.

## Design features
- Corporate branding and typography
- Product category presentation
- QR code integration
- Print-oriented layout

## Deliverables
- Editable SVG artwork
- High-resolution PNG preview
